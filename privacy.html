Information We Collect

Information you give us. Your name, email address, mobile phone number, and anything else you choose to include when you fill out a form, apply to work with Maura, join a mailing list, or write to us.

Information collected automatically. Standard technical data such as your browser type, device, and the pages you visit on this site.

We do not collect payment card details on this website. Payments are processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

To respond to your enquiries and applications.

To send you the emails and text messages you have asked to receive, including course information, event details, and offers.

To deliver the programs and services you enroll in.

To improve this website and our offerings.

To meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

Text Messaging and Your Mobile Data

If you give us your mobile number and consent to receive text messages, we use it only to send you the messages described in our Text Message Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and our messaging platform, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us.

Email and Text Message Platforms

We use Klaviyo to send email and text messages. Klaviyo processes your contact details on our behalf and is bound by its own privacy commitments.

Every marketing email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link and our mailing address.

Cookies

This website uses only the cookies necessary for the site to function. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on it.

How We Protect Your Information

We limit access to your information to the people and services that need it to do the work described above, and we hold it no longer than we need it.

Client session content is treated as strictly confidential and is never used for marketing of any kind.

Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to us at the address above and we will act on your request.

You may unsubscribe from email at any time using the link in any email, and from text messages by replying STOP.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

This website and our programs are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy we will post the revised version here and update the effective date at the top of the page.
