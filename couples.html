Behind Every Door

Good Isn’t the Goal. Extraordinary Is.

The masculine is the banks. The feminine is the water. No banks? The river floods. No water? It dries up. Together - flow. Most couples were never taught this simple architecture of love, so they fight the river or starve it.

Relationship Magick weaves attachment science, somatic practice, and the tantric arts of polarity into one path. Not another date night - a deeper practice. You will not just repair what frayed; you will build something the two of you have never had before.

A small circle of committed couples, mentored by Maura over several months. Polarity. Repair. Devotion. Truth-telling. Fifteen couples only - then the doors close.
