These are not passive Zoom calls. You’ll be gazing into each other’s eyes, breathing together, and engaging in powerful guided practices designed to create safety, spark, and exquisite attunement.

You’ll learn to

The In-Person Intensive

This weekend is pure immersion. You and your partner will practice in person, gazing into each other’s eyes, breathing together, dropping into exercises that restore erotic charge, emotional safety, and magnetic presence. The sexual energy in the room will be palpable, and you’ll feel more turned on by your partner than you ever have before.

Between sessions, you’ll be sent on intentional dates: opportunities to practice magnetism in the real world. You’ll learn how to hold your polarity not just in private, but while navigating a restaurant, a city street, a shared meal. You’ll see what it means to stay connected and alive in life, not just in the container.

You’ll leave with practices in your bodies, not just in your minds, ready to create a love that’s expansive, rooted, and undeniably alive.

Meet Your Guide

Maura Grace

Maura Grace is a relationship expert and tantrika who holds a Master’s degree in Transpersonal Counseling Psychology from Naropa University. She has spent over a decade in post-master’s training in couples therapy, trauma therapy, somatic therapy, and masculine/feminine studies.

Having trained with Stan Tatkin, David Deida, Peter Levine, and other brilliant minds, Maura has also studied indigenous wisdom and shamanic traditions with Martín Prechtel. She has spent over 20 years facilitating transformational work with powerful, high-caliber men and women.
