Text Message Terms

The terms of our text message program.

Effective August 5, 2026

The Program

Maura Leigh Grace, LLC, operating as Maura Grace Wisdom, offers a text message program that sends course announcements, event and retreat information, enrollment details, offers, and occasional teaching notes.

The program is operated in the United States and Canada.

Consent

You are enrolled only if you gave us your mobile number and agreed to receive text messages, whether on a sign-up form, at an event, or through another opt-in we offer.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing anything from us.

By joining you confirm you are the account holder or have the account holder’s permission, and that you are 18 or older.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You can expect roughly 2 to 6 messages per month, and more during an enrollment period or in the days around an event.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for the messages themselves; your mobile carrier may charge you according to your plan.

How to Stop

Reply STOP to any message from us to be removed from the program. You will receive one confirmation message and then nothing further.

You can rejoin at any time by opting in again.
